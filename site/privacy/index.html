Privacy in plain language.

Receipt to Room keeps household paperwork on your device.

What stays local

Receipt text is read by files bundled with the app. Images are not uploaded and are discarded after reading. Reviewed records, a paid-version token, and its recent result stay in local app storage. Spreadsheet, printable, and backup files are created only when you ask.

What leaves the device

When you restore the paid version, the app sends its token to Sociobot to check it. Payment opens on Sociobot. We do not receive receipt images or inventory.

Tracking and retention

The app has no analytics, telemetry, advertising, crash reporter, or third-party runtime scripts. This website sets no analytics cookies. Local records remain until you remove them or clear app data.

Your choices

You can export records, delete items, or remove app data through your operating system. For questions, email privacy@sociobot.in.
